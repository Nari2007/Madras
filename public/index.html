MADRAS

Born from the streets, MADRAS is an OG Tamil RP community built on loyalty, brotherhood, and unforgettable stories. We don't just play roleplay. 03 / Community guidelines

Respect the roleplay.

Starter guidelines — check Discord for the official rules and requirements.

Respect every player

Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not welcome.

No RDM or VDM

Do not randomly attack players or use vehicles to harm players without valid roleplay context.

No metagaming or powergaming

Keep outside information separate from your character and allow others a fair chance to respond.

No cheating or exploiting

Do not use unauthorized modifications or abuse bugs. Report issues to staff.
